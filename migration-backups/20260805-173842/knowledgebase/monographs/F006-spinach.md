# F006 - Spinach

**Knowledge Base ID:** F006\
**Scientific Name:** *Spinacia oleracea*\
**Common Names:** Spinach\
**Food Group:** Vegetables (FG003)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Spinach is a nutrient-dense leafy green rich in folate, vitamin K, vitamin A, magnesium and lutein. It provides exceptional nutritional value with very few calories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Spinach provides an exceptional concentration of micronutrients and protective plant compounds that support cardiovascular, metabolic and long-term health.

## Avoid If

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose crisp, vibrant leaves.

## Storage

Refrigerate and use within a few days.

## Preparation

Wash thoroughly before eating or cooking.

## Shopping Tips

Avoid wilted or yellowing leaves.
