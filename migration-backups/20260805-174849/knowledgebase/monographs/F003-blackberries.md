# Overview

Blackberries are richly pigmented berries providing fibre, vitamin C, vitamin K and manganese. They are among the highest-fibre commonly consumed fruits and are an excellent source of polyphenols.

They are particularly valuable for digestive health, cardiovascular wellbeing and healthy ageing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Blackberries are an excellent whole-food choice for supporting cardiovascular health, healthy ageing, gut health and reducing dietary reliance on ultra-processed snacks.

# Active Compounds

## Anthocyanins

Powerful plant pigments associated with vascular health, antioxidant activity and healthy ageing.

Research suggests they may:

- Support endothelial function
- Reduce oxidative stress
- Support healthy cognitive ageing

## Flavonoids

Research suggests they may:

- Support healthy inflammatory balance
- Promote healthy blood vessels

# Health Benefits

## Cardiovascular Health

Supports healthy blood vessels and circulation.

## Brain & Nervous System

May contribute to healthy cognitive ageing.

## Digestive Health

Dietary fibre supports a diverse gut microbiome.

## Metabolic Health

May contribute to healthy glucose regulation within a balanced diet.

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose ripe, brightly coloured fruit free from mould.

## Storage

Refrigerate and consume within a few days.

## Preparation

Rinse immediately before eating.

## Shopping Tips

Frozen fruit retains most nutritional value.
