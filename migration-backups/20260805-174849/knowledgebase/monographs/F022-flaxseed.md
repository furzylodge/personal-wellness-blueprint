# F022 - Flaxseed

**Knowledge Base ID:** F022\
**Scientific Name:** *Linum usitatissimum*\
**Common Names:** Flaxseed\
**Food Group:** Nuts & Seeds (FG005)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Flaxseed is one of the richest dietary sources of lignans and alpha-linolenic acid, supporting cardiovascular and digestive health.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Flaxseed provide nutrient-dense healthy fats, plant protein and bioactive compounds that support cardiovascular, metabolic and brain health as part of a balanced diet.

# Health Benefits

## Cardiovascular Health

Supports healthy cholesterol and vascular function.

## Brain & Nervous System

Provides essential fats and micronutrients supporting normal nervous system function.

Fibre supports a healthy gut microbiome.

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose raw or minimally processed products.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container in a cool place.

## Preparation

Eat raw, soaked or lightly roasted as appropriate.

## Shopping Tips

Avoid heavily salted or sugar-coated varieties.
