# FD017 - Kidney Beans

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD017\
**Scientific Name:** *Phaseolus vulgaris*\
**Common Names:** Kidney Beans\
**Food Group:** Legumes (FG004)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Kidney beans are an excellent source of fibre, plant protein and minerals, supporting healthy blood sugar regulation and digestive health.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Kidney Beans are excellent whole foods that combine high-quality plant nutrition with fibre and slowly digested carbohydrate. Regular consumption is associated with improved cardiovascular, metabolic and digestive health.

# Active Compounds

## Resistant Starch

Research suggests it may:

- Feed beneficial gut bacteria
- Increase short-chain fatty acid production
- Support healthy glucose metabolism

## Polyphenols

Research suggests they may:

- Reduce oxidative stress
- Support cardiovascular health
- Promote a healthy gut microbiome

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Health Benefits

## Cardiovascular Health

Supports healthy cholesterol and vascular health.

## Digestive Health

Provides fibre and prebiotic compounds supporting microbiome diversity.

## Metabolic Health

Supports healthy glucose regulation and satiety.

## Musculoskeletal Health

Provides plant protein and minerals supporting normal body function.

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose dried or low-salt canned varieties.

## Storage

Store dried legumes in airtight containers.

## Preparation

Soak dried beans where appropriate and cook thoroughly.

## Shopping Tips

Choose products with no added salt where possible.
