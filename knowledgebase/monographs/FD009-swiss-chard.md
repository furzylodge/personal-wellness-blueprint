# FD009 - Swiss Chard

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD009  
**Scientific Name:** *Beta vulgaris subsp. vulgaris*  
**Common Names:** Swiss Chard  
**Food Group:** Vegetables (FG003)  
**Evidence Level:** High  
**Status:** Approved

---

# Overview

Swiss chard provides vitamins K, A and C together with magnesium and colourful betalain pigments that contribute antioxidant activity.

---

# Why It Matters

Swiss Chard provides an exceptional concentration of micronutrients and protective plant compounds that support cardiovascular, metabolic and long-term health.

## Avoid If

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose crisp, vibrant leaves.

## Storage

Refrigerate and use within a few days.

## Preparation

Wash thoroughly before eating or cooking.

## Shopping Tips

Avoid wilted or yellowing leaves.
