# Seaweed

## Identity
- Scientific name: Marine macroalgae
- Food group: Other Functional Foods

## Key canonical bioactives
- Chlorogenic Acid (`PP008`) is retained only as a conservative secondary mapping because seaweed species vary substantially and chlorogenic acids are not universal dominant constituents.

## Other important constituents
Seaweeds can provide iodine, fucoidans, alginates, phlorotannins and other minerals and polysaccharides. These are not currently represented as canonical PWB bioactives.

## PWB relevance
Seaweed is primarily being added as a candidate route into the thyroid/mineral and marine-polysaccharide space. Iodine should remain in the separate nutrient layer, and fucoidans/phlorotannins should be considered only after a constituent-level review.

## Evidence
High for the broad constituent classes; exact composition is strongly species-dependent.
