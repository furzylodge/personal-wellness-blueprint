# FD018 - Walnuts

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD018\
**Scientific Name:** *Juglans regia*\
**Common Names:** Walnuts\
**Food Group:** Nuts & Seeds (FG005)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Walnuts are unique among commonly eaten nuts for their high alpha-linolenic acid (ALA) omega-3 content, together with polyphenols, vitamin E and copper.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Walnuts provide nutrient-dense healthy fats, plant protein and bioactive compounds that support cardiovascular, metabolic and brain health as part of a balanced diet.

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose raw or minimally processed products.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container in a cool place.

## Preparation

Eat raw, soaked or lightly roasted as appropriate.

## Shopping Tips

Avoid heavily salted or sugar-coated varieties.
