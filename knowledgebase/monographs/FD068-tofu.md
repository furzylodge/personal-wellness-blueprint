# FD068 - Tofu

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD068\
**Scientific Name:** *Glycine max* (processed)\
**Common Names:** Tofu, Bean Curd\
**Food Group:** Beans & Legumes (FG004)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Tofu is made by coagulating soy milk and pressing the resulting curds into a solid block, a process similar in principle to cheesemaking. It retains soybeans' complete protein profile and isoflavone content, with the coagulant used (often a calcium or magnesium salt) frequently making tofu a good source of calcium as well.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Tofu is one of the most versatile whole soy foods, retaining much of soybeans' nutritional strength:

-   Provides a complete plant protein, comparable in quality to soybeans
-   Often calcium-set, making calcium-set tofu a useful plant-based calcium source
-   Contains isoflavones, with a well-studied safety profile at typical dietary intakes
-   Neutral flavour and texture that readily absorbs seasoning, supporting wide culinary use

# Active Compounds

## Complete Protein

Tofu retains soybeans' complete amino acid profile, making it a valuable protein source within vegetarian and vegan diets.

## Isoflavones

As with whole soybeans, tofu contains isoflavones studied for cardiovascular and menopausal health effects, with a generally reassuring safety profile at typical dietary intakes according to major food safety and health bodies.

## Calcium (from coagulant)

Tofu set using a calcium-based coagulant (calcium sulfate) can provide a substantial calcium contribution; tofu set with a magnesium-based coagulant (nigari) will be lower in calcium, so checking the label or product type matters for those relying on tofu as a calcium source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Health Benefits

## Plant-Based Protein Support

Tofu's complete protein profile makes it a versatile protein source within plant-based diets, comparable in protein quality to animal sources.

## Bone Health (Calcium-Set Varieties)

Calcium-set tofu can meaningfully contribute to calcium intake, relevant for those with reduced dairy consumption.

## Cardiovascular Health

As a whole soy food, tofu is associated with the same modest favourable cardiovascular associations seen with soy protein more broadly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Contraindications

**Avoid if:**

-   Confirmed soy allergy

**Use caution if:**

-   Relying on tofu specifically for calcium intake, check whether the product is calcium-set, as magnesium-set (nigari) tofu is considerably lower in calcium
-   Taking thyroid medication, where separating soy food intake from medication timing by a few hours is generally advised

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose:

-   Firm or extra-firm tofu for stir-fries, grilling, and baking; silken tofu for smoothies, desserts, and sauces
-   Calcium-set varieties (check the ingredients label for calcium sulfate) if using tofu as a calcium source

## Storage

Store unopened tofu per packaging (often shelf-stable until opened); refrigerate opened tofu in water, changing the water daily, and use within a few days.

## Preparation

Pressing firm tofu before cooking removes excess water and improves texture; suitable stir-fried, baked, grilled, or blended (silken) into sauces and desserts.

# Common Myth

**Myth:** All tofu contains the same amount of calcium.

**Fact:** Calcium content depends heavily on the coagulant used; calcium sulfate-set tofu is considerably higher in calcium than tofu set with a magnesium-based coagulant (nigari), so checking the ingredients list matters for anyone using tofu as a calcium source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Key Takeaway

Tofu is a versatile, complete-protein soy food that can also be a valuable plant-based calcium source depending on how it was coagulated, with a well-established safety and nutrition profile.
