# FD070 - Chicken Breast

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD070\
**Scientific Name:** *Gallus gallus domesticus*\
**Common Names:** Chicken Breast, Skinless Chicken Breast\
**Food Group:** Meat & Poultry (FG008)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Chicken breast is a lean poultry meat and one of the most widely consumed sources of complete animal protein worldwide. Skinless chicken breast is particularly low in saturated fat compared with other meats, while providing a full complement of essential amino acids together with several B vitamins and trace minerals.

Its mild flavour, versatility and favourable macronutrient profile make it a staple protein source across a wide range of dietary patterns, from general healthy eating to muscle-building and weight-management diets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Regular, moderate consumption of lean poultry as part of a balanced diet is associated with:

-   High-quality, complete protein intake
-   Support for muscle maintenance and repair
-   A lower saturated fat alternative to red meat
-   Meaningful contributions of niacin, vitamin B6 and selenium
-   Support for healthy immune and metabolic function

Because it is comparatively low in fat and calories relative to its protein content, chicken breast is frequently recommended within weight-management and sports nutrition guidance.

# Active Compounds

## Complete Protein

Chicken breast provides all nine essential amino acids in proportions the body can use efficiently, making it a "complete" protein source comparable to other lean animal proteins.

## Selenium

An essential trace mineral involved in antioxidant defence (via selenoproteins such as glutathione peroxidase) and thyroid hormone metabolism.

## Niacin and Vitamin B6

B vitamins involved in energy metabolism, and in the case of B6, amino acid metabolism and neurotransmitter synthesis.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Health Benefits

## Muscle Maintenance

The high-quality protein in chicken breast supports muscle protein synthesis, relevant to both general muscle maintenance and recovery after exercise.

## Metabolic Health

As a lean protein source, chicken breast can support satiety and help displace higher-fat or more processed protein sources within an overall dietary pattern.

## Immune and Thyroid Support

Selenium content contributes to normal immune function and thyroid hormone metabolism.

# Allergen Information

Chicken is not one of the recognised major food allergens, though poultry allergy, while rare, can occur.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Contraindications

**Avoid if:**

-   Confirmed poultry allergy

**Use caution if:**

-   Following a diet requiring restricted purine or protein intake for a specific medical condition (seek individual clinical advice)

Undercooked or improperly stored poultry carries a risk of bacterial contamination (e.g. Campylobacter, Salmonella); thorough cooking and safe handling are essential.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose:

-   Fresh or frozen chicken breast from a reputable source
-   Skinless cuts for a lower saturated fat option

## Storage

Refrigerate raw chicken and use within the use-by date, or freeze for longer storage. Store separately from other foods to avoid cross-contamination.

## Preparation

Cook thoroughly until no longer pink and juices run clear (internal temperature of at least 74°C/165°F). Suitable for grilling, baking, poaching and stir-frying.

# Common Myth

**Myth:** All chicken meat has the same nutritional value.

**Fact:** Skinless breast meat is substantially leaner than thigh or leg meat with skin, which contain considerably more fat.

# Key Takeaway

Chicken breast is a widely accessible, lean, complete-protein food that fits comfortably into most dietary patterns, providing meaningful amounts of niacin, vitamin B6 and selenium alongside high-quality protein.
