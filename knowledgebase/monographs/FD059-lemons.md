# Lemons

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Citrus limon*
- Food group: Fruit

## Key canonical bioactives
- Hesperidin (`PP009`)
- Limonene (`TP001`)

## Candidate future constituent
- Eriocitrin is characteristic of lemon but is not currently in the canonical bioactive catalogue. It is therefore not added to the taxonomy at this stage.

## PWB relevance
Lemon expands citrus coverage while reusing established canonical constituents. Its specific flavonoid profile includes hesperidin and other citrus flavonoids.

## Evidence
High for food identity and occurrence of citrus flavonoids; no new canonical constituent is created from this monograph.
