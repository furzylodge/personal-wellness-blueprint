# Carrots

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Daucus carota* subsp. *sativus*
- Food group: Vegetables

## Key canonical bioactives
- Alpha-carotene (`CA005`) is the primary new constituent relationship in the PWB taxonomy.
- Carrots also contain other carotenoids, but this monograph does not create additional relationships unless supported by the canonical model.

## PWB relevance
Carrots provide a direct food source for alpha-carotene, helping close the current carotenoid coverage gap.

## Evidence
High for the food identity and established dietary occurrence of carotenoids; constituent relationships should remain conservative.
