# Artichoke

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Cynara cardunculus* var. *scolymus*
- Food group: Vegetables

## Key canonical bioactives
- Chlorogenic Acid (`PP008`)
- Lignin (`DF008`)

## Other important constituents
Artichoke contains characteristic caffeoylquinic acids including cynarin. Cynarin is not currently canonicalised, so it is recorded as a future constituent candidate rather than added at this stage.

## PWB relevance
Artichoke provides an additional whole-food source of chlorogenic acid and reinforces the plant-fibre/polyphenol layer. Its distinctive hepatic/biliary potential should not be promoted to a food-level mechanism until the relevant constituent relationships are established.

## Evidence
High for the characteristic phenolic profile; constituent abundance depends on plant part and preparation.
