# FD077 - Almond Milk

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD077\
**Scientific Name:** *Prunus dulcis* (beverage)\
**Common Names:** Almond Milk, Almond Drink\
**Food Group:** Plant-Based Alternatives (FG013)\
**Evidence Level:** Moderate\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Almond milk is a plant-based dairy alternative made from ground almonds and water. It is naturally low in calories and protein compared with dairy milk, and commercial products are typically fortified with calcium and vitamins D, E and B12.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Almond milk is a popular dairy alternative, particularly among those seeking a lower-calorie option:

-   Naturally very low in protein compared with dairy milk or soy milk
-   Fortified varieties provide calcium, vitamin D, vitamin E and vitamin B12
-   Naturally contains a small amount of vitamin E from almonds

# Active Compounds

## Vitamin E

Naturally present in almonds and carried through in modest amounts to almond milk; an antioxidant nutrient supporting cell protection.

## Fortificants (Calcium, Vitamin D, Vitamin B12)

Added during manufacturing in most commercial products; unfortified almond milk is very low in these nutrients.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Health Benefits

## Dairy-Free, Lower-Calorie Alternative

Provides a lower-calorie plant-based option for those with lactose intolerance, cow's milk protein allergy, or following a vegan diet, particularly useful within calorie-controlled eating patterns.

## Antioxidant Support

Naturally occurring vitamin E contributes modest antioxidant activity.

# Allergen Information

Almonds (tree nuts) are one of the fourteen major EU/UK allergens requiring mandatory labelling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Contraindications

**Avoid if:**

-   Confirmed tree nut allergy

**Use caution if:**

-   Relying on almond milk as a significant protein source, as it is naturally very low in protein compared with dairy or soy milk
-   Relying on it as a primary calcium/vitamin D/B12 source without checking it is fortified

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose:

-   Fortified, unsweetened varieties for closer nutritional parity with dairy milk without added sugar

## Storage

Refrigerate after opening and use within the recommended timeframe.

## Preparation

Suitable for drinking, in tea/coffee, cereal, baking and smoothies.

# Common Myth

**Myth:** Almond milk is a good source of protein.

**Fact:** Almond milk is naturally very low in protein (often under 1g per 100ml) compared with dairy milk (~3.4g) or soy milk (~3g); it should not be relied upon as a significant protein source.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Key Takeaway

Almond milk is a low-calorie, dairy-free alternative that, when fortified, provides useful calcium, vitamin D and B12, but should not be relied upon as a protein source.
