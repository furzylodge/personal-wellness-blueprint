# FD002 - Blueberries

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD002\
**Scientific Name:** *Vaccinium corymbosum*\
**Common Names:** Blueberries, Highbush Blueberries\
**Food Group:** Fruit (FG002)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Blueberries are small, nutrient-dense berries recognised for their exceptionally high concentration of polyphenols, particularly anthocyanins. They provide fibre, vitamin C, vitamin K and manganese while contributing relatively few calories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Blueberries have one of the strongest evidence bases among fruits for supporting cardiovascular health, healthy ageing and cognitive function. Their combination of fibre and phytochemicals also contributes to gut microbiome diversity and healthy metabolic function.

# Active Compounds

## Anthocyanins

These pigments give blueberries their deep blue colour and are associated with antioxidant activity and vascular health.

Research suggests they may:

- Support endothelial function
- Reduce oxidative stress
- Support healthy cognitive ageing

## Flavonoids

Support normal cell signalling and inflammatory balance.

Research suggests they may:

- Support healthy blood vessels
- Help maintain normal inflammatory responses

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Health Benefits

## Cardiovascular Health

Supports vascular function and healthy blood flow.

## Brain & Nervous System

Evidence suggests regular blueberry intake may support memory and cognitive performance.

## Digestive Health

Provides fibre that supports a healthy gut microbiome.

## Metabolic Health

May support healthy glucose regulation as part of a balanced diet.

# Contraindications

## Avoid If

- Confirmed blueberry allergy

## Use With Caution

- Follow medical advice if taking warfarin due to vitamin K intake.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose firm, deep-blue berries with a natural bloom.

## Storage

Keep refrigerated and consume within one week.

## Preparation

Rinse immediately before eating.

## Shopping Tips

Fresh and frozen blueberries provide similar nutritional value.

# Common Myth

**Myth**

Only fresh blueberries are healthy.

**Fact**

Frozen blueberries retain most nutrients and polyphenols.
