# FD007 - Kale

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD007\
**Scientific Name:** *Brassica oleracea var. sabellica*\
**Common Names:** Kale\
**Food Group:** Vegetables (FG003)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Kale is one of the most nutrient-dense vegetables available, providing vitamins K, C and A together with fibre and glucosinolates.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Kale provides an exceptional concentration of micronutrients and protective plant compounds that support cardiovascular, metabolic and long-term health.

# Contraindications

## Avoid If

- Confirmed allergy.

## Use With Caution

- People taking warfarin should maintain a consistent vitamin K intake.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Practical Guidance

## Buying

Choose crisp, vibrant leaves.

## Storage

Refrigerate and use within a few days.

## Preparation

Wash thoroughly before eating or cooking.

## Shopping Tips

Avoid wilted or yellowing leaves.
