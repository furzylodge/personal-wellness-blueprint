# Grapes

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Vitis vinifera*
- Food group: Fruit

## Key canonical bioactives
- Proanthocyanidins (`PP013`)
- Resveratrol (`PP006`)
- Anthocyanins (`PP001`)

## PWB relevance
Grapes close the current food-source gap for proanthocyanidins and provide an important food source for the existing grape-associated polyphenols resveratrol and anthocyanins.
