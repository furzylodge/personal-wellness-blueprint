# Mandarins

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Citrus reticulata*
- Food group: Fruit

## Key canonical bioactives
- Hesperidin (`PP009`)
- Limonene (`TP001`)

## PWB relevance
Mandarin adds another distinct citrus food and a useful source of the existing canonical flavanone hesperidin. This strengthens the food-level representation of an already established constituent without expanding the canonical bioactive set.

## Evidence
High for food identity and citrus occurrence of hesperidin and limonene.
