# Eggs

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Gallus gallus domesticus*
- Food group: Animal Foods

## Key canonical bioactives
- Lutein (`CA002`)
- Zeaxanthin (`CA003`)

## Other important constituents
Eggs are also a dietary source of choline and several vitamins and minerals. Choline is not currently part of the PWB canonical bioactive catalogue, so no new constituent has been created here.

## PWB relevance
Eggs introduce an animal-food source into the model and provide an additional food route for the existing carotenoid constituents lutein and zeaxanthin.

## Evidence
High for food identity and carotenoid occurrence. Nutrient relationships remain in the separate nutrient layer.
