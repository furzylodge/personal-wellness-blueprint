# Pomegranate

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Punica granatum*
- Food group: Fruit

## Key canonical bioactives
- Anthocyanins (`PP001`)
- Ellagic Acid (`PP007`)
- Proanthocyanidins (`PP013`)

## Other important constituents
Pomegranate is especially rich in hydrolysable tannins including punicalagins. Punicalagins are not currently canonicalised in the PWB model, so they are recorded as a future constituent candidate rather than added as a new bioactive.

## PWB relevance
Pomegranate materially strengthens the polyphenol food layer and provides a useful source for ellagic acid and anthocyanins, while also reinforcing the newly established proanthocyanidin coverage.

## Evidence
High for the characteristic polyphenol profile; constituent concentrations vary by cultivar, part and processing.
