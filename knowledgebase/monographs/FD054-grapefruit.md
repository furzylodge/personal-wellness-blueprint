# Grapefruit

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Citrus × paradisi*
- Food group: Fruit

## Key canonical bioactives
- Naringenin (`PP010`)
- Limonene (`TP001`)

## PWB relevance
Grapefruit introduces a clear citrus source for naringenin and also provides a food source for the existing citrus terpene limonene.

## Evidence
High for the characteristic occurrence of these citrus constituents. Food-drug interaction considerations are outside the constituent taxonomy and should be handled separately.
