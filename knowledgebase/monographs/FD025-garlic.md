# FD025 - Garlic

**Knowledge Base ID:** FD025\
**Scientific Name:** *Allium sativum*\
**Common Names:** Garlic\
**Food Group:** Whole Foods (FG006)\
**Evidence Level:** High\
**Status:** Approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Overview

Garlic contains organosulphur compounds, particularly allicin, and has one of the strongest evidence bases among culinary herbs for cardiovascular support.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Why It Matters

Garlic is recognised as an evidence-supported whole food that contributes important nutrients and bioactive compounds to a healthy dietary pattern.
