# Oranges

## Identity
- Scientific name: *Citrus sinensis*
- Food group: Fruit

## Key canonical bioactives
- Hesperidin (`PP009`)
- Limonene (`TP001`)

## PWB relevance
Orange adds a major citrus source for the existing canonical flavanone hesperidin and the citrus terpene limonene. Hesperidin is well established as a citrus flavonoid and has been studied in metabolic, cardiovascular, inflammatory and neurological contexts. The PWB taxonomy should retain the existing mechanism mappings rather than create additional claims solely because orange contains hesperidin.

## Evidence
High for food identity and citrus occurrence of hesperidin; clinical effects of orange juice/hesperidin remain dose- and context-dependent.
