## Methodological decisions

- Do not add questions merely because a health topic exists.
- Require a unique, actionable signal to justify a new question.
- Do not confuse correlation with independent scoring evidence.
- Do not convert context into health severity.
- Do not make diagnostic claims from questionnaire responses.
- Preserve evidence provenance and distinguish historical rationale from modern evidence.

## Evidence trail requirement

For the final freeze, each retained/changed question should have:

- construct
- decision
- evidence rationale
- source references
- evidence scope
- scoring role
- recommendation consequence
- final wording

The project already has an evidence/applicability specification requiring source, review date and evidence scope, and explicitly distinguishes historical rationale from modern evidence.
