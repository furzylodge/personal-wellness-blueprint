PERSONAL WELLNESS BLUEPRINT · 2026

A calmer way to answer the questions

These six examples exercise the reusable question components: goal, frequency, multi-select with conditional follow-up, quality, activity, and medical context.

YOUR GOAL

Would you like to have more energy during the day?

This records your stated priority. It does not itself indicate poor health.
DIGESTIVE & GUT HEALTH

How often do you have difficulty digesting certain foods?

A higher response contributes to the Digestive & Gut Health signal.
HEALTH CONTEXT

Do you currently have, or have you previously been diagnosed with, any of the following skin conditions?

FOLLOW-UP
How much does this skin condition currently affect your day-to-day life?
This is context only. A diagnosed skin condition is not treated as a general health deficit.
SLEEP & RECOVERY

How would you describe the quality of your sleep?

This contributes directly to Sleep & Recovery.
ACTIVITY

On how many days in a typical week do you do around 30 minutes or more of moderate or vigorous activity?

Think about activities such as brisk walking, cycling, swimming, running or sport. It does not have to be one continuous session.

The UK Chief Medical Officers recommend adults aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity each week, or 75 minutes of vigorous activity, or a combination. Activity can be spread across the week and shorter bouts count. This question uses days as a simpler way to describe a typical pattern.
PERSONAL CONTEXT

Is there any diagnosed health condition or ongoing treatment that you would like your practitioner to be aware of?

This is optional. It gives your practitioner useful context when discussing your results. It does not form part of your wellness signals.

OPTIONAL CONTEXT
Which areas would you like your practitioner to be aware of?

Select any that apply. These broad categories are for context only. You do not need to provide a diagnosis or detailed medical history.

This information is optional and is provided as context for your practitioner. It does not form part of your wellness signals and does not constitute a medical assessment.
