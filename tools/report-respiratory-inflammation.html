How we choose your foods

We remove any food from your recommendations that conflicts with an allergy or dietary preference you told us about — so a peanut allergy means peanuts (and anything made from them) never appear here, even if they would otherwise be a strong match for your results.

Vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian and no red meat are based on each food’s actual animal content — vegan also removes dairy and eggs, pescatarian keeps fish but not meat or poultry.

Gluten-free and dairy-free remove any food already flagged as containing gluten or milk — the same check used for allergies.

Low-FODMAP is more of a judgement call than the others. How much FODMAP a food contains genuinely depends on portion size, which we don’t model yet, so we’ve erred on the side of caution: a food is excluded if it’s commonly a trigger at a typical serving, even where a smaller amount might be fine. Treat this as a helpful starting point rather than a clinically supervised low-FODMAP plan.

Halal and kosher don’t currently change your results — none of the foods in our database conflict with either (there’s no pork, shellfish or alcohol-based ingredient yet). We’ll expand this as more foods are added.
